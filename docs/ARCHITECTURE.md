# Crescendo — Platform Architecture

Crescendo is an intelligent, private-first musical learning, creativity,
practice, recording and performance platform.

The platform is being designed as a long-term musical environment rather
than a single-purpose examination application.

---

## 1. Product Vision

Crescendo brings together:

- Music learning
- Instrument practice
- Musicianship
- Aural training
- Sight-reading
- Theory
- Composition
- Recording
- Performance
- Intelligent coaching
- Musical progress tracking

The first major experience is Mihira's ABRSM Grade 1 Piano Coach.

The platform will progressively expand beyond ABRSM into a complete musical
learning and creativity environment.

---

## 2. Core Experience Layers

### Learn

Structured musical education:

- ABRSM preparation
- Music theory
- Musical terms
- Aural skills
- Sight-reading
- Rhythm
- Ear training
- Scales and arpeggios
- Musical vocabulary

### Play

Instrument interaction:

- Virtual piano
- Piano practice
- Microphone input
- Pitch detection
- Rhythm and timing analysis
- Future MIDI keyboard support
- Future external instrument integrations

### Create

Creative musicianship:

- Composition
- Melody creation
- Rhythm creation
- Improvisation
- Musical experiments
- Creative missions

### Record

Personal studio:

- Audio recording
- Performance
