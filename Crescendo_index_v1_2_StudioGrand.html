What's real here, and what isn't. Pip and the games are genuinely measuring Mihira through your microphone — pitch, timing and loudness — that's real signal analysis, not a script, and it's reliable for the aural echo games (right note? on time?) and for dynamics/crescendo shaping (how loud, how gradual). What it isn't: an official ABRSM examiner. There's no automatic way to check whether every note of a full piece was correct, so piece performances get objective pulse/dynamics measurements plus a rubric for you or her teacher to score by ear, the way a real exam would. Treat scores here as encouraging, honest practice feedback — not a certified grade.
